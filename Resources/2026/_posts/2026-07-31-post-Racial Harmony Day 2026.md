<p>What a celebration it was! Last week, our school marked Racial Harmony
Day with a burst of colour, culture and community — from ketupat weaving
and rangoli art to dazzling dance performances. Our CCE Ambassadors and
Wellness Champs kept the energy high with interactive booths, games and
Conversation Boxes in collaboration with the Singapore Kindness Movement,
sparking curiosity and meaningful connections across cultures. Our Parent
Support Group added a special touch, immersing students in authentic cultural
experiences through traditional ethnic games, saree draping and henna.
It was a joyful reminder of the beautiful diversity and friendships that
make Singapore home.</p>
