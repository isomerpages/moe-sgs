<p>From coding and live orchestral music to pastry making, automotive engineering,
and careers exploration, our Sec 1 to 3 students enjoyed a whirlwind of
authentic, hands-on experiences throughout LSP2. Whether they were cracking
code, rolling dough, discovering Singapore's story, acquiring financial
literacy, or imagining their future selves, every activity sparked curiosity,
built real-world skills, and deepened connections — with their interests,
their community, and one another. Across all three levels, as learning
came alive, students didn't just learn about the world; they stepped into
it, led with heart, and grew together in ways that no textbook alone could
offer.</p>
