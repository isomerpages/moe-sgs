<p>From 23 to 27 July, SGS hosted six students and a teacher from Hong Kong's
Tang Shiu Kin Victoria Government Secondary School SGS for the milestone
20th Singapore-Hong Kong Exchange Programme. The exciting week kicked off
with a lively Kompang performance and a warm welcome reception. The visiting
guests fully immersed themselves in school life alongside their SGS buddies
and friends, by attending classroom lessons, joining Racial Harmony Day
celebrations, and going on a learning journey to the Singapore Discovery
Centre.</p>
